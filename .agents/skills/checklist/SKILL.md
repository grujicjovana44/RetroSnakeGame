# Checklist

- Convert findings from `analyze` into checkable items.
- Include scope, source-of-truth, acceptance, test, evidence, and review gates.
- Mark each item as `PASS`, `FAIL`, `OPEN`, or `NOT APPLICABLE`.
- Do not mark runtime or browser behavior as complete without evidence.
- Keep unresolved decisions visible for `clarify`.
