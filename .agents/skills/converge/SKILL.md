# Converge

- Reconcile the analysis, checklist, clarified decisions, and constitution.
- Produce one concise decision record: chosen behavior, rejected alternatives, scope, risks, and acceptance boundary.
- Resolve contradictions by source-of-truth precedence, never by silent editing.
- Require human confirmation when the decision changes scope or a public contract.
- Pass only a stable decision record to `specify`.
