# Constitution

- Derive principles from confirmed requirements, repository constraints, and existing `AGENTS.md` rules.
- Define source-of-truth precedence, scope boundaries, quality gates, testing, evidence, and change control.
- Keep principles stable and project-level; do not put feature-specific details here.
- Record amendments explicitly with rationale and approval.
- Do not weaken security, validation, review, or no-secret rules to make implementation easier.
